About SEENRO

An independent initiative, starting small and building in the open.

SEENRO Technology exists to solve real problems in Sierra Leone first, and to grow beyond it once the foundations are solid.

What we want to build

  • Independent infrastructure. Our own backend foundation, APIs and developer services, so the people building on them can understand and control what they depend on. This work has started as SEENRO Core.
  • Defensive AI and security tools. Practical tools that help developers and organizations find weaknesses and detect attacks. This is a research direction, not a product.

How we work

  • Affordable and practical before impressive.
  • Secure by default, with security claims backed by tests.
  • Honest about status: every feature is marked as working, in development or planned.
  • Open to developers, researchers, universities and organizations who want to collaborate.

Where we stand

SEENRO is at an early stage and is built by its founder in Sierra Leone. We do not yet have a public product or customers to announce, and we will not suggest otherwise.
