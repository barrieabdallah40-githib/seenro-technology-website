Research direction

Defensive security and practical AI.

Security and AI are longer-term areas of exploration for SEENRO Technology. No standalone AI model or security product is currently released. This page describes direction, not finished tools.

Areas of interest

Problems worth solving

Vulnerability awareness

Research into AI-assisted identification and explanation of software weaknesses.

Threat detection

Exploring ways to identify suspicious activity and automated attacks.

Practical AI

Investigating useful AI applications for developers and businesses in Sierra Leone.

Responsible development

Measure before release.

  • Keep work defensive, lawful and responsible.
  • Prioritise affordable computing and suitable existing models where practical.
  • Evaluate accuracy, false positives and operational risks before releasing tools.
  • Test changes on systems we own or are authorised to assess.

Current work

Security begins with the foundation.

Current security engineering is part of SEENRO Core's development, including credential protection, access controls, request limits and automated tests. These controls are ongoing engineering work, not a claim that the platform is risk-free.
