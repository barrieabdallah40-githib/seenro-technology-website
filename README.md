# SEENRO Technology website

Public website for SEENRO Technology, an independent technology company based in Sierra Leone. The company is building practical software, digital systems and backend infrastructure, with a long-term ambition to develop broader cloud and digital infrastructure services for businesses, institutions, developers and individuals.

## Website pages

- `index.html` — Company homepage
- `solutions.html` — Technology solutions and areas of focus
- `about.html` — Company mission and vision
- `core.html` — SEENRO Core
- `security-ai.html` — Security and AI
- `contact.html` — Contact information
